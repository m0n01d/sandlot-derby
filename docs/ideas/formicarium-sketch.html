Formicarium — sketch

The playable sketch formicarium.md is written from. Self-contained: the JavaScript below is the reference for the core's sand automaton, dig plan, ants, brood, starvation and catch-up. Time runs at ×1200 by default so a farm-day passes in about a minute; the app runs at ×1.

Feed them and watch a forager carry the crumb down to the store. Drag the gravity slider and the sand pours to whichever side is down; on a phone, gravity follows how you hold it. Starve them and the timeline is real ant keeping: the workers eat the eggs first, then die one a day, then the queen; the sand keeps the tunnels for the next queen.
