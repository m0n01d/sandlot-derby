Darkroom — sketch

The playable sketch darkroom.md is written from. Self-contained: the JavaScript below is the reference for the core's slosh, development, dither and verdict functions. Drag left and right across the tray to rock it, or use the arrow keys; tilt works on a phone. Your own photo never leaves the page.

The shadows come up first, as on real paper. Rock evenly and the print is even. Leave it tilted and one side runs dark. The lights are a hard cut, and the print comes out warm-toned in five of the sixteen colours with a Bayer dither.
